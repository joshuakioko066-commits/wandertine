EMAIL FORM NOTE:
The contact form uses FormSubmit because the site is static and has no server-side mail handler.
On the first live submission, FormSubmit may send a one-time activation/confirmation email to info@wandertine.co.ke.
After activation, future submissions will be delivered to that mailbox.
If the live domain differs from wandertine.co.ke, update the _next value in contact.html and sitemap/robots URLs accordingly.

IMAGE NOTE:
The site continues to use Unsplash-hosted imagery, as the original build did. Images were selected for semantic fit and visual variety. People-focused imagery is used where the content calls for travelers or business teams; imagery was not artificially inserted into sections where destination/landscape photography better serves the page.
